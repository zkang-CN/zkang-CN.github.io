# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

This site serves domestic and international scholars, potential collaborators, academic committees, recruiters, and students who need to understand Kang Zheng's research profile quickly.

## Product Purpose

The website is a bilingual personal academic name card. It presents Kang Zheng's identity, research focus, education, selected publications, representative projects, academic service, and contact path in a credible and easy-to-scan format.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, geospatial.

The site should feel academic and reliable, with a restrained visual signature connected to GeoAI, remote sensing, road networks, and environmental exposure mapping.

## Anti-references

The site should not look like a dark futuristic AI product, a generic resume template, a card-heavy SaaS landing page, or a decorative portfolio page. It should avoid oversized marketing hero copy, dense bilingual paragraphs on the same screen, heavy gradients, and loud technical effects.

## Design Principles

- Put research identity first: visitors should understand the AI + geospatial direction within 30 seconds.
- Keep the interface readable: publications, dates, affiliations, and project roles must be easy to scan.
- Use real academic content: the page should foreground actual papers, education, projects, and service.
- Add one quiet geospatial signature: fine spatial lines or coordinate motifs can create identity without reducing credibility.
- Protect public privacy: use email for contact and do not expose the phone number from the private resume.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target practical WCAG AA readability. Maintain sufficient text contrast, visible keyboard focus, responsive mobile layout, language toggle support, descriptive image alt text, and reduced-motion handling.
